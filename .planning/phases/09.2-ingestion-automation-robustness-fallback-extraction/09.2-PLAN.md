## Stacking-Error Minimization

- Keep every plan mergeable: one behavioral change at a time, one verification script.
- Maintain a single green smoke path after every plan:
  - `POST /dev/wipe` -> `POST /ingest` -> `POST /ingest/{id}/extract` -> `GET /ingest/{id}`
- When adding a worker service, validate end-to-end with a single "choker" PDF before broadening.
